## Live client work

**SPAAMFAA membership platform.** A national antique fire apparatus association replaced its legacy membership system with a platform I built: online dues, officer tools, and role-based permissions for 72 officers and trustees. Went live 5 October 2026 with 3,221 members and 22,288 payments migrated after seven practice runs. [members.spaamfaa.org](https://members.spaamfaa.org)

**TrailWorks booking platform.** A mountain bike coaching school moved from texts, emails and a calendar to online booking, waivers, payments, reminders and two-way Google Calendar sync. The owner: *"What used to take me about 8 to 10 minutes to do per client now takes me about 15 seconds."* [See the booking calendar](https://trailworks-platform.vercel.app/programs)
